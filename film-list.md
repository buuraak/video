## In progress

**Film 1: Tomorrow** (2:40)

- **Theme:** stuck in a depressive cycle, unable to start working on yourself.
- **Big visual idea:** the day keeps resetting to 6:00 and the same morning replays. Each loop is shorter and the clock later. No enemy figure: the villain is his own "I'll start tomorrow" and the red clock. He breaks out through visible action: running, the gym, journaling, a whiteboard with 5 easy actions, talking with people. (Dropped: a plant he waters. Too quiet; viewers want to see action.)
- **Story:** beat by beat, with the scene table, in [stories/tomorrow.md](stories/tomorrow.md). The whiteboard starts with huge goals (make $1,000,000, a Bugatti, a yacht, a marathon, a six-pack) and after the turn holds five easy things (get up at the alarm, clean your room, go for a walk, write 3 lines, text one person). "I'll start tomorrow" is a caption only, no spoken voice.

Built so far (the story's first scenes; the rest is in the story file):

- Scene 1, waking up (`scenes/wake_up.py`, about 20 s). Fade in from black. From straight above: he sleeps on the nightstand side of the bed (one pillow, under his head; the other half of the bed is empty), blanket to his chest, hands resting on his belly on top of it. Nightstand left of the pillow with the alarm clock (5:59) and his phone on the charger. He's restless: worried brows, head rolling slowly, uneven breathing. The camera creeps in a little. Cut to the clock from the front, filling the screen: 5:59, then 6:00, the red blocky numbers blink with harsh beeps. His stick arm drags in from the right and presses the button heavily; the beeping stops. Cut to his face from above: eyes open slowly to half, heavy lids, bags. Sound: a low dark hum under everything, the alarm beeps, the click of the button.
- Scene 2, scrolling (`scenes/scroll.py`, 12 s, cut down from 17). Hard cut back to the wide view from above, as at the start of scene 1; the camera holds that position (gentle wobble only). The clock stays 6:00. Only his arm moves to grab the phone off the nightstand and unplug it; he holds it up over his chest: we see the back of the phone and its cold blue-white light flickering on his tired face as reels play (about 4 s, 2 swipes). The reels sound muffled: a different person in each, with quiet music under some, cutting and changing with each swipe. They are ElevenLabs voices selling the same huge dream as his whiteboard: Adam on his first million, Laura on a six-pack in 30 days, Liam on his Bugatti and yacht (the long one, still playing on the bed as he gets up). Feedback: the first version used the Mac's voices, which the user found weird even muffled; the ElevenLabs version was approved. He drops the phone on the empty half of the bed, still playing, throws the blanket toward the nightstand side, sits up, shuffles across the empty half, gets out on the right and walks toward the right edge, seen truly from above. Footsteps. The shortening kept his slow, tired pace and dropped moments instead (a third swipe, pauses), and the scene cuts as he reaches the edge; the board scene picks him up arriving.
- Scene 3, the board (`scenes/the_board.py`, 10 s, story row 6). From the side, the wall by the bedroom door: he shuffles in from the left, tired, head drooping, and stops at the whiteboard (a dark board with a faint shine, the door beside it), lifts his head to it and sags. Hard cut to the board from the front, filling the screen: NEW ME, the five huge goals, START: MONDAY. His stick arm comes up from below left (the side he stands on), takes the red marker from the tray by its cap end, lets it hover over MONDAY for a moment, crosses it out in one heavy stroke, puts it back, takes the pale marker, slowly writes TUESDAY under it, letter by letter, and puts that back. Caption: "I'll start *tomorrow*.", coming up as he grabs the red marker and staying fully up until the cut (the user's choice). Feedback: the first version (6 s) was "way too fast"; it now takes 10 s, every move slow and heavy, with time to read the goals. The 10-second version was approved. Sound: the hum, his shuffling steps, the markers clicking on the tray and squeaking on the board (ElevenLabs effects).
- Scene 4, night (`scenes/night.py`, 5 s, story row 7). Close-up of his phone from above, lying on the nightstand in the dark: it buzzes twice and the lock screen lights up, 22:47, and Sam's message slides in: "you coming tonight?" (the lock-screen look was picked from three options). Hard cut to the opening view of the bedroom from above, at night, the clock at 22:47: he lies awake, his face lit cold blue on the side nearest the phone, reaches over slowly and turns it face down; its light leaks round the edges for a moment and the room goes dark. His hand stays on it; he breathes out, blinks and looks back up at the ceiling. Sound: the hum, the phone buzzing on the wood (one long ElevenLabs buzz cut into a double buzz), a soft knock as it lands face down. Approved as first built.
- Scene 5, the reset (`scenes/reset.py`, 4 s, story row 8). The alarm clock from the front, the same shot as scene 1's alarm: 23:59 in the dark for a second, then the numbers race backwards through the night and the evening, faster and faster with a fading red trail, and slam onto 6:00 with a camera jolt and a thud. The alarm goes off and keeps ringing as the scene ends. Sound: the hum, an ElevenLabs tape rewind played faster and faster so it rises in pitch, cut dead at the slam, then the alarm. The user picked this from a preview of two versions (rejected: the numbers glitching and tearing) and liked it as built.
- The quote (`scenes/the_quote.py`, 5 s, story row 7b, between the night and the reset).
